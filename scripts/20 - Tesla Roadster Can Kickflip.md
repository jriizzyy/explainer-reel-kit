# 20 - Tesla Roadster Can Kickflip

Thesis: Tesla finally showed the new Roadster on October 15, 2026, and it does stunts nobody asked for: flips, hops, sticking upside down. The specs are wild, the price and date are missing, and this is the same car that was supposed to be on sale in 2020. Tone: slightly condescending, like explaining it slowly to someone who already put a deposit down.

Facts checked 2026-10-16 (see Sources). Notes on the brief:
- There are three versions, not two: Performance, Thrust (limited) and Rocket (invite only, one seat, not road legal).
- The Roadster was first shown in November 2017, not 2020. 2020 was the promised production year.
- "Production in 2028" was not announced by Tesla. Asked about it on stage, Musk said "2028, actually." No price, no official date.

## Hook

"Tesla just unveiled a car that can do a kickflip. A car. Doing a kickflip."

## Spoken lines

1. Tesla just unveiled a car that can do a kickflip. A car. Doing a kickflip.
2. Last night Tesla finally showed the new Roadster. Before you get excited, let me walk you through it. Slowly.
3. First thing. There isn't one Roadster. There are three.
4. Performance is the one you can actually drive on a road. Thrust is limited, with thrusters on the back. And Rocket is invite only, one seat, and not legal on public roads.
5. The flip is the party trick. Cold-gas thrusters, built with SpaceX, flip the whole car from a standstill. Nobody was inside, obviously.
6. And Rocket? That's literally just the rocket car from GTA. Press a button, it boosts, it hops over traffic. Except this one costs real money.
7. Here's the spec sheet. You don't need me to read it to you. Just look.
8. The big number is zero to sixty in 0.59 seconds. That's the claim. The live run was 0.81, and there was no timer on screen. So, trust me, bro.
9. And it "flies." Calm down. The thrusters fire in short bursts. It's a very expensive hop.
10. Production? Elon said 2028. Not a press release, not a date on the website. Somebody asked, and he said "2028, actually." That's the whole commitment.
11. No price either. But reservations are back. Fifty thousand dollars. Five now, the other forty-five within ten days, or you lose your spot.
12. Quick history lesson, since apparently we need one. Tesla first showed this Roadster in 2017. It drove out of the back of a semi truck. Six hundred twenty miles of range, four seats, on sale in 2020.
13. 2020 came. No Roadster. Then it was 2023. Then this unveil slipped from April, to August, to October first, to October fifteenth.
14. And the version that finally showed up has half the range and half the seats.
15. So yes, it can kickflip. It can hop like a GTA car. It just can't hit a deadline. 2028, actually. Sure. We'll see.
